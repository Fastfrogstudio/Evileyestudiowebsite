Who We Are

Artists who happen to make slots

Founded in 2020, Evil Eye Studio was built by animators, mathematicians, and lifelong gamers who refused to settle for cookie-cutter slots. We obsess over the feel of a spin — the anticipation, the tension, the payoff.

That same craft powers our studio-for-hire, where operators and other providers bring us their concepts and we make them move.
